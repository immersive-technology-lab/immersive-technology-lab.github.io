Augmented Reality Action Summaries for Situated Instructions of Repetitive Tasks

Poster paper at ISMAR 2026
Lucchas Ribeiro Skreinig1, Ana Stanescu1, Peter Mohr1, Christoph Ebner1,
Markus Tatzgern2, Dieter Schmalstieg3, and Denis Kalkofen1
1Graz University of Technology, 2Salzburg University of Applied Sciences, 3University of Stuttgart

Abstract

Augmented reality (AR) instructions help people understand how to operate or assemble objects by overlaying visual guidance directly onto the physical environment. However, repeatedly presenting detailed instructions can negatively impact performance. We introduce an interactive AR instruction system that groups instructions based on recurring action patterns, providing detailed guidance on the first occurrence of actions and abstracting repeated sequences. We introduce a novel workflow that automatically organizes narrated expert demonstrations into instruction structures using a multimodal large language model, improving the scalability of AR guidance systems. Our user study shows that grouped instruction visualization is clearly preferred.
